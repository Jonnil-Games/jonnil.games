About Us

We’re a small group of folks who love making games that feel genuine, welcoming, and just plain fun.

Who We Are

We started Jonnil Games because we wanted a space where creativity and honesty take the lead. Every game we make is something we truly care about—no stuffy corporate talk, just a bunch of people working together to bring playful ideas to life.

What Matters to Us

These guiding principles shape every game we create and every step we take. They’re our reminders to stay honest, keep evolving, and welcome everyone who wants to join in.

Accessibility

We’re all about making sure everyone can jump in and have fun, regardless of background or abilities. Your comfort and enjoyment matter to us.

Player-Centric Design

It starts with you. We focus on what feels right, what’s natural, and what sparks joy for actual players, not just checkboxes.

Community

We’re better together. Hearing your thoughts, sharing ideas, laughing over inside jokes—community keeps our worlds alive and ever-changing.

Authenticity

No fluff, no trying too hard. We keep it real, heartfelt, and honest, because at the end of the day, we’re here to have fun and share something meaningful.

Diversity & Inclusion

Different voices bring richer stories. We embrace a range of backgrounds and perspectives, weaving that variety into every world we create.

Willingness to Try New Things

We love to experiment—tinkering with new ideas, wild mechanics, or unexpected art styles. Sure, we might stumble, but that’s how we discover something truly special.

Our Journey Forward

We’re not aiming for perfection, just to keep it real. We’ll keep listening, experimenting, and putting you at the heart of it all. If you stick around, we hope to surprise you with experiences that spark imagination, conversation, and curiosity. We’re happy you’re here.
