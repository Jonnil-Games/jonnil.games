Spook: The Asylum

Prepare to step into a narrative-driven horror platformer where nothing is quite as it seems. On the surface, a seemingly straightforward horror tale unfolds—but hidden within are layers of intricate puzzles, secret easter eggs, and multiple endings. Drawing inspiration from classic RPGMaker indie horror games and reimagining the raw ideas of Spook: The Clinic, this game challenges you to uncover its dark, twisting mystery on your own terms.

🕯️

Immersive Horror

Delve into a world where every shadow and eerie sound builds a chilling atmosphere. Our game captures the classic indie horror vibe with a modern, refined twist.

🕹️

Challenging Platforming

Jump, dash, and navigate treacherous obstacles in levels designed to test your reflexes and keep your heart racing.

🧩

Puzzles & Hidden Secrets

Every corridor and crumbling wall hides clues—solve intricate puzzles and unearth secret easter eggs that reveal the asylum’s mysterious past.

📜

Branching Narratives

Your choices carve your fate. Experience multiple endings and divergent story paths that invite you to explore the deeper, dark narrative at your own pace.

Current Development Status

Spook: The Asylum is in active development, and we’re hard at work blending the classic charm of indie horror with modern platforming gameplay. Heavily inspired by RPGMaker story-driven titles, this game takes the raw ideas of Spook: The Clinic and refines them into an experience that’s as much about uncovering hidden lore as it is about heart-pounding action.

At first glance, you’ll encounter a straightforward horror tale—but dive deeper, and you’ll find a labyrinth of puzzles, secret areas, and branching storylines that change with every decision you make. We’re retooling old concepts with fresh storytelling, innovative game design, and improved programming to deliver a haunting mystery that you must piece together on your own.
