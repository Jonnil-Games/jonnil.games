## Added

- Information > Backups explains how to protect your work. Screenshot PNGs save the picture, not editable history or a whole server. It also covers host backups and browser storage limits.
- On supported desktop builds, Information > Backups has shortcuts to save-data, screenshots, character and stamp folders. Close the game before copying a host's save-data folder.
- Information > Community includes a Discord link and tips for sharing work and reporting problems.
- Information > About > Privacy and data explains session sharing, host logs, local saves and Discord activity settings.
- Information > Credits lets you read game credits and bundled third-party licenses and notices. Long credits scroll automatically and can be paused.

## Changed

- Information now uses tabs and adapts to smaller windows.
- Scroll long Information pages with the mouse wheel, Page Up/Down, Home/End or Up/Down buttons. To Top returns to the start.
