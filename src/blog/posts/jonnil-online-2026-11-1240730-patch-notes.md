## Added

- Optional accounts on Windows. Open Account from the main menu to create an account or sign in. No email address is needed. Guest play remains available, and browser builds use guest play.
- While signed in, your display name and built-in character and pet choices save automatically to your account for use across Windows devices. Custom artwork stays on this device. Account shows save progress, offers "Retry cloud save" if saving pauses, and lets you choose which profile to keep if devices disagree.
- Guest and account profiles stay separate. Use Account > Copy guest profile to copy your guest display name and built-in character and pet choices into your account. Signing out returns to your guest profile.
- The Windows sign-in screen offers "Keep me signed in" for 30 days or until you sign out. Leave it off on shared devices.
- New accounts receive a private recovery code. Save it and use Account > Recover account if you forget your password. Losing both your password and recovery code means losing access.
- Account > Account settings lets you export account data, replace your recovery code, sign out or delete your account. Deletion removes your account and cloud profile but keeps your guest profile. Older backups may remain.

## Changed

- The player name field is now "Display name" in the main menu and server browser, separate from your private sign-in username.
- Drawing toolbar and layer tooltips use larger text, wrap across lines, and expand to fit the explanation.
- Drawing layer limit messages explain when to archive a layer, press A to remove an empty layer, or ask the server owner for help.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue that could stop other players from receiving new public drawing layers and layer management changes.
- Fixed missed Ctrl+V and Shift+Insert pastes when Ctrl or Shift is released quickly.
