## Added

- Browser players can sign in through Account to restore their saved profile on supported HTTPS pages. Guest play remains available. Export your data at Account > Account settings > Export account data > Download account data.
- Options > Display adds GUI Scale: Auto or 100%-200% in 25% steps. It saves on this device and scales menus, text and information screens without changing camera zoom. Smaller windows may reduce the scale to keep controls visible.
- Options > Display adds Fullscreen. In browsers, use F10 or Ctrl/Cmd+F on Mac. Escape exits browser fullscreen. Gamepads cannot toggle browser fullscreen.

## Changed

- "Keep me signed in" keeps browser sign-in for up to 30 days or until you sign out. Privacy settings or clearing site data can end it sooner. With it off, reloading can still restore sign-in for up to 12 hours. Sign out on shared devices.
- Account forms now show character counts and input rules, with a Show password option. Correct invalid or overlong entries before submitting. If text was cut off, clear the field and enter it again.

## Fixed

- Fixed pointer alignment for browser menu buttons, drawing controls and tool cursors when the game is resized or fullscreen.

## Security

- Older browser tabs now block profile saves if another tab switches accounts, preventing changes from being saved to the wrong account.
