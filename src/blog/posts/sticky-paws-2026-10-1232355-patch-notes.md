## Added

- Desktop launch shortcuts to the world map (-world-map), Level Editor level list (-level-editor), online levels (-online-levels), and character selection (-characters). Add one to the game's launch options.
- Open Options > Video at startup with -options -options-page=video. Audio, Accessibility, and other available pages can also be selected.
- Open an existing local custom level with -edit-level=FOLDER or -play-level=FOLDER. Replace FOLDER with its folder name, not a path. When editing, add -editor-grid=show or -editor-grid=hide to show or hide the grid.
- Start a built-in level with -official-level=ID, using its level ID. Demo limits still apply.
- With gameplay shortcuts, add -character=CHARACTER_FOLDER to choose Player 1's character and -players=2, -players=3, or -players=4 to join more players automatically.
- Shortcuts run once per launch. Invalid launch requests fall back to the title screen. Online levels and Options pages keep their usual access requirements.
