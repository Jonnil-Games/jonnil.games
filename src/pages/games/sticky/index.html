Join Sticky the Cat on a delightful adventure to reclaim a stolen cake from mischievous mice! Navigate through 8 charming levels filled with challenges and fun, where you can climb walls, pounce through cardboard boxes, and play with friends in this physics-based platformer that combines solo fun with multiplayer mayhem.

Gameplay Videos

👥

Multiplayer Fun

Play with up to 4 players locally or online using Steam's Remote Play Together! Only one player needs to own the game to invite friends for multiplayer fun.

🐱

Playable Characters

Choose between Sticky and Catlyn, each with their unique charm! The game supports custom character additions for even more variety.

🎮

Creative Level Editor

Design and share your own levels! Create challenging courses, export them, and share with friends. Import levels from others for endless gameplay possibilities.

🏆

Competitive Features

Challenge friends with high scores and speedruns! Each level tracks your best performance, letting you compete for the fastest times and highest scores.

Available Now on Steam!

Experience the joy of sticky adventures solo or with friends! Sticky Paws combines precise platforming with physics-based mechanics, letting you climb walls, pounce through obstacles, and navigate challenges however you like at your own pace.

With the included level editor, your creativity knows no bounds. Create your own challenges, share them with friends, and discover new levels made by the community. Whether you're competing for the best times, designing levels, or just having fun with friends, there's always something exciting to do in Sticky Paws!
