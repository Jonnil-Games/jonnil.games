STICKY PAWS

Game Introduction

Sticky the Cat is on a little adventure to retrieve a cake that a mouse stole. Help Sticky through 8 levels full of dangerous mice. Go about the adventure however you like at your own pace.

Game Features

  • Multiplayer with up to 4 players! You can play locally or you can use Steam's built-in Remote Play Together, where only one player needs to own the game and 3 other Steam friends can play together online!
  • 2 Characters, Sticky and Catlyn! More custom characters can be added!
  • Level Editor! Make a level and then export the level, and send the level to other players so they can play your level! Or your friends can make a level for you to play, that you import into the game!
  • Fun movement, like climbing walls and pouncing through cardboard boxes!
  • High score and Speedrun! Each level keeps track of your highest score and fastest time!
  • Compete with your friends and see who has the highest score or fastest time on each level and try to beat theirs!
